# Bootstrap: discovery, group 1

## Step 2.1: Group 1 - What is it?
Read: bootstrap/ONBOARD-DISCOVERY.md; bootstrap/RED-FLAGS.md § Discovery Turn Budget
Produces: what the product does and for whom, template or product, ship or learn, and the owner's answer to the one look question
Check: evidence: the owner's answers to this group in their own words, quoted; a question an earlier answer settled is recorded as inferred, with the inference; a question the owner declined is recorded as declined, with what was assumed
Skip by: owner

- What does your app do? Describe it like you're explaining to a friend.
- Who uses it? Just you? Your team? The public?
- Can you name an existing app that's similar to what you want? (even loosely)
- Is this a product you want to ship, or a project whose purpose is to learn a specific technology? Record either purpose as a normal owner goal. A learning goal does not make cost, safety, or operational constraints disappear.
- Is this a template or starter that other projects consume, or the end product its users interact with? Record the answer here. Step 4 applies the corresponding artifact mechanics.
- For a product, ask once: do you have ideas about how it should look, or should I choose what fits your business? Preserve anything the owner mentions. The design interview later gathers the detail and records the direction; this question does not choose a look.

If the opening names infrastructure that appears disproportionate to the stated audience, do not infer why. Ask whether using or learning that technology is part of the goal. The answer, not the apparent mismatch, determines how Step 3 evaluates it.
