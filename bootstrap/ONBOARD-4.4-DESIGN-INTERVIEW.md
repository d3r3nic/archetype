# Bootstrap: the design interview

## Step 4.4: The design interview
Read: bootstrap/DESIGN-INTERVIEW.md; templates/design-interview/index.html; #27 § Design tools; #29
Produces: the answers file in the design working-files folder; the direction or directions shown; the decision recorded at the decision location as picked or owner-delegated; the brand book; or, when the owner has not answered yet, the form offered and the pick recorded as open
Check: evidence: the owner's pick or delegation in their own words with the decision record's location, or that the form was offered, when, and that the pick is still open
Depends on: bootstrap.4.2; bootstrap.4.3; bootstrap.2.3
Basis: decisions and inputs required
Skip when: the build approach is a platform, the project is a template, or the product has no screen
Skip by: owner

The interview runs here, after the build approach and the operating stage are settled, so nobody answers questions about the look of a product that turns out to need no custom screen. It is a form with gates that the session opens for the owner, with two ways through it: the owner's pick from shown directions, or the owner's delegation in words (#29); an `isolated` project is offered the short way first (#30). Everything about how it is asked lives in `bootstrap/DESIGN-INTERVIEW.md`.

## Record what later work depends on

Close with `--basis` for each accepted decision underlying the brief and `--input` for the answers file. Once picked or delegated, include the direction decision and its artifact input. An open pick is recorded honestly; only the already settled brief is an accepted basis. On a skipped path, record the build/template/no-screen decision and its input rather than a visual decision that never happened. The tool records the input fingerprints; do not copy the brief into the ledger.
