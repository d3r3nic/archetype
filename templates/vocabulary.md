# Vocabulary

Every thing the product has, named once, with the verb that acts on it (#31 "Words"). Navigation, headings, buttons, errors, and messages use these words and no others. It starts from the owner's own words (the design interview's `words` answer); the session adds the verbs. A second name for one thing anywhere in the product is a violation; fix the product or fix this file, never both ways at once.

| Thing | We call it | Verbs | Never call it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [what it is, in plain words] | [the one name] | [create, edit, archive, send] | [names the owner hates; system-side names] | [plural; how it appears in a sentence] |

## Verbs that are easy to confuse

- [Remove takes it out of a list; Delete destroys it. The product uses both only if it has both.]
- [Save keeps changes; Send delivers to someone else.]
